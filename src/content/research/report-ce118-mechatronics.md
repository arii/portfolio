# CMPE-118 An Introduction to Mechatronics: MAK Attack

## Autonomous Robotic Platform at UC Santa Cruz

Developed for **CMPE-118 Introduction to Mechatronics** at UCSC (with Matthew Luxton and Kyle Huey), the **MAK Attack** project engineered an autonomous mobile robot designed to execute complex arena navigation and object manipulation tasks under real-time constraints.

---

## Technical Design & Mechatronic Integration

The robot combined custom mechanical chassis design, sensor signal conditioning, and event-driven embedded C programming.

Key technical components:
1. **Hierarchical State Machines:** Hierarchical state machines for robust tape-following, arena navigation, and target acquisition.
2. **Custom Sensor Integration:** Integrated IR optical tape sensors, bumper hit detectors, and optical encoders with hardware debounce circuitry.
3. **Actuation & Power Management:** Designed H-bridge motor drive circuits with PWM speed regulation and battery voltage monitoring.
