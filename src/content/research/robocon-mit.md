# RoboCon MIT

## Fostering Cross-Departmental Robotics Collaboration

**RoboCon MIT** was a cross-departmental robotics research conference organized to bridge research silos between the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL), Mechanical Engineering (MechE), AeroAstro, and Media Lab robotics groups at MIT.

---

## Objectives & Community Role

While MIT hosts world-class robotics research laboratories, inter-laboratory communication across departments was historically fragmented. RoboCon was established to:
1. Provide a unified forum for PhD researchers and postdocs to showcase ongoing hardware and algorithmic breakthroughs.
2. Facilitate interdisciplinary collaboration across perception, manipulation, controls, and biomechanics groups.
3. Offer undergraduate students hands-on exposure to cutting-edge robotics research labs.

---

## Technical Infrastructure & Conference Coordination

Ariel led technical web platform development and conference organization:

### 1. Digital Platform & Registration Pipeline
- Developed and maintained the conference web platform for abstract submissions, speaker schedules, and interactive poster session maps.
- Implemented automated registration tracking and speaker management tooling.

### 2. Program Curation & Keynote Symposia
- Coordinated multi-track speaker sessions covering autonomous vehicles, soft robotics, humanoid control, and learning-based manipulation.
- Organized live hardware demonstration halls enabling research groups to demonstrate experimental robotic platforms to industry sponsors and peers.

---

## Impact

- Successfully connected hundreds of student researchers, faculty members, and robotics industry partners across New England.
- Established a repeatable organizational playbook for student-led technical conferences at MIT.
